NeuroSoma • Architecture Map

Application Tour & Code Architecture

NeuroSoma is an interactive 3D clinical anatomy study companion and proactive knowledge-gap audit tool designed for medical and healthcare trainees.

1. System Architecture & Execution Flow

The system couples a real-time Three.js 3D anatomical model with an asynchronous FastAPI backend, a dual-layer knowledge-gap auditor, and a structured notes engine with dual persistence: student study notes in localStorage, and verified clinical audit records in the audit_notes/ directory.

2. Core Functional Route (The Audit & Persistence Loop)

Follow one concrete action through its complete client-server lifecycle:

Stop 1: Audit Trigger & Knowledge-Gap Detection
static/js/audit.js • triggerAudit() → engine/auditor.py • audit_notes()

When the student clicks Audit Notes or types audit, the frontend bundles all current notes and submits them to POST /api/audit. The auditor checks each anatomical structure in data/anatomy_graph.json against student keywords. The first uncovered structure is flagged as a knowledge gap and paired with a dynamically selected clinical scenario from a pool of 3 distinct questions per structure.

Stop 2: Smooth Camera Glide & 3D Transparency Isolation
static/js/viewport.js • glideToTarget(), selectStructureById()

Upon gap detection, the 3D camera interpolates smoothly (via spherical/quaternion glide) to align directly with the target structure's anatomical position. All unselected meshes dim to 25% opacity while the target structure remains fully opaque, visually isolating the anatomy before the "What-If" clinical scenario modal opens.

Stop 3: Evaluation, Companion Feedback & Dedicated Disk Saving
engine/what_if.py • evaluate_what_if(), save_audit_record() → audit_notes/

Submitting the student's clinical assessment invokes POST /api/what-if. The companion evaluates the answer against high-yield clinical indicators (e.g., trapezius impairment for CN XI). The complete audit record (question, answer, companion feedback, verified takeaway) is saved to the audit_notes/ directory as both JSON and Markdown (<id>_audit.md). The record is simultaneously added to the left hierarchy tree under Clinical Audit Notes with a direct View Audit Note action.

4. A Practice to Reuse

Dual-Layer State Partitioning: Separating unvetted drafts from verified records.

In NeuroSoma, free-form student study notes live in browser localStorage (discarding blank drafts), while formal clinical assessments evaluated by the companion are saved directly to disk in audit_notes/ as structured JSON and readable Markdown.

This pattern ensures lightweight local responsiveness during freeform exploratory study, while guaranteeing durable, version-controllable, portable clinical audit records that can be exported, graded, or reviewed across sessions.
