NeuroSoma: Anatomy & Knowledge-Gap Navigator

A persistent study companion for anatomy and biomedical coursework that pairs an interactive anatomical viewer with an active gap-detecting auditor and clinical "what-if" reinforcement.

The NeuroSoma Feedback Loop Architecture
The Unique Kernel

Dynamic Diagnostic Navigation + "What-If" Reinforcement:

Traditional anatomy apps either show a 3D model or present static flashcards. NeuroSoma reads your own study notes, cross-references them with structural ground truth, and physically maneuvers the camera directly to your blind spots.

Once the gap is corrected, it poses a functional "what-if" clinical scenario (e.g. nerve compression or tendon rupture) to cement why the structure actually matters.

Who It's For & Why It Matters

The Biomedical / Pre-Med Student:

Sitting down for long study blocks with lecture slides open in one window and a persistent tab in another. Needs to actively test whether their mental map is complete before an exam.

The Stake: Anatomy is too vast to rely on passive slide re-reading. Having a proactive AI assistant that catches omitted connections in your notes builds clinical intuition faster.

The 5-Step Core Loop
1

Explore & Toggle

Load the segmented view (nervous & musculoskeletal); toggle layers to observe how nerve pathways weave through muscles and bones.

2

Inspect & Inquire

Click a structure or type into the terminal to query its function, innervation, and knowledge status (Known vs. Unknown).

3

Annotate in Context

Write your study notes and explanations directly in the study pane as you learn the segment.

4

Trigger the Audit

Click "Audit Notes"—the tool scans what you wrote, locates an unmentioned or thin structure, moves the viewport directly to it, and drops a gap callout.

5

Reinforce with "What-If"

Update your note to address the gap; the tool responds with a clinical "what-if" scenario that tests real physiological consequence.

Proof of Concept Boundary
  • ✓
    Segmented Nervous & Musculoskeletal View: A dedicated cervical/cranial or limb anatomical segment with toggleable nerve and muscle layers.
  • ✓
    Interactive Viewport with Camera Pan: Dynamic focal transition that smoothly pans/rotates to highlight specific anatomical coordinates.
  • ✓
    Dual Interaction: Click-to-inspect part selection combined with a terminal/chat prompt input.
  • ✓
    Personal Note Console: Space for the student to write notes in their own words.
  • ✓
    Audit Engine + Anchored Gap Callouts: Automated scan of student notes against segment ground truth, triggering camera movement and callouts.
  • ✓
    Clinical "What-If" Scenario Trigger: Presents a functional question after resolving a knowledge gap to test applied clinical logic.
  • ⏳
    Full-Body System Coverage: Cardiovascular, digestive, endocrine, and reproductive systems across the entire human body.
  • ⏳
    Dual 2D/3D Mesh Switching: Seamless toggle between high-res histological slices and full textured 3D CAD meshes.
  • ⏳
    Spaced Repetition Scheduler: Long-term flashcard decay curves and multi-session spaced retrieval algorithms.
  • ⏳
    User Account & Progress Persistence: Cloud login, synced cross-device storage, and persistent database profiles.
  • ✕
    Full-Body Rigging & Muscle Deformations: Unnecessary technical complexity for proving active note-auditing.
  • ✕
    Native Mobile App Packaging: Out of scope for this hackathon; the desktop browser persistent tab is the primary interface.
  • ✕
    Multiplayer / Study Group Collaboration: Single-player focus keeps student privacy intact and scope tightly controlled.
