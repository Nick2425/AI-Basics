The 7-Step Verified Journey
Detailed Feature Requirements & Acceptance Criteria

Interactive Viewport with 1:1 Synchronized ViewCube: Both the 3D cube and anatomical viewport rotate in real time together.

  • ✓
    1:1 Rotation Synchronization: Dragging the ViewCube directly rotates the anatomical model inside the viewport; dragging the canvas rotates the ViewCube in unison.
  • ✓
    Functional Orthogonal Snapping: Clicking any face (Front, Back, Right, Left, Top, Bottom) snaps both the cube and anatomical model to that exact vantage point.
  • ✓
    Uncluttered UI: Zero helper text or redundant captions beneath the cube or viewport controls.
  • ✓
    Application View Menu Bar: Layer selection is configured through a classic top menu (`View > Layers > [x] Nervous, [x] Musculoskeletal`) with checkmarks.
  • ✓
    Transparency Dimming Isolation: Selecting an anatomical structure or auditing a gap dims all surrounding structures to 25% opacity while the target structure remains at 100% full brightness.

Subtle Sticky Notes: Anchored callouts with soft, understated borders avoiding harsh or obnoxious outlines.

  • ✓
    Refined Visual Styling: Dark glassmorphism cards with subtle 1px border (`rgba(255,255,255,0.09)`) and soft elevation shadows.
  • ✓
    Structure Selection: Clicking a nerve or muscle opens an anchored sticky-note overlay with its clinical tag, name, and note text.
  • ✓
    Dynamic Tree Addition: Entering notes adds the structure into the left-hand navigation tree.
  • ✓
    Blank Note Discard: Empty notes are discarded upon closing and never saved as blank drafts, keeping the tree uncluttered.

Proactive Note Auditor & Camera Glide: Politeness meets automation—prompts when ready, then physically guides attention.

  • ✓
    Readiness Prompt: After notes are updated, the terminal offers: "Ready to audit your notes? [Accept] [Decline]".
  • ✓
    Camera Glide & Dimming: On accept, the viewport camera glides to center on the omitted structure while dimming surrounding parts.
  • ✓
    Temporary Highlighting: Terms and concepts under audit are temporarily highlighted in yellow inside the note.

Clinical What-If Challenge: Turns gap correction into applied clinical logic.

  • ✓
    Modal Challenge: A clean overlay asks a physiological scenario (e.g. nerve lesion, entrapment, or biomechanical failure).
  • ✓
    Interactive Response: The user types their clinical answer and submits.
  • ✓
    Auto-Appended Insight: The validated clinical insight is appended directly to the bottom of the structure's note.

Consequential States & Boundaries:

  • ✓
    First Launch: Pre-loaded with exactly 1 sample note (e.g. Vagus Nerve) to demonstrate format without overwhelming empty slots.
  • ✓
    Empty Notes State: If audited with 0 notes, outputs: "No notes found to audit. Inspect a structure and jot down some thoughts first."
  • ✓
    100% Thorough State: When all concepts are covered, audits older notes using spaced what-if retrieval rather than inventing fake errors.
1:1 Rotation Synchronization
Rotating either the ViewCube or the anatomical canvas drives both simultaneously, ensuring complete spatial alignment without lag.
Subtle Note Aesthetics
Eliminates obnoxious high-contrast outlines in favor of soft, refined dark cards that integrate smoothly with the clinical UI.
View Dropdown Menu
Modeled after desktop IDEs/Sheets (`View > Layers`), providing a standardized menu interface rather than floating pill buttons.
Transparency Dimming
Surrounding structures drop to 25% opacity when a part is selected or audited, cleanly isolating the anatomy without drawing circles.
Strict No-Emoji Standard
Exclusively professional clinical typography and iconography across all text, terminal outputs, and buttons.
